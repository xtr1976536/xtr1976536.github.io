This project formulates financial volatility forecasting as a passive probabilistic world-model problem. A causal HAR-IV anchor models the predictable level, while a recurrent state-space model learns market-shared and asset-specific innovation dynamics. The project evaluates forecast accuracy, calibration, and joint path realism separately.

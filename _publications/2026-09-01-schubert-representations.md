This project studies structured representations for unordered collections of fixed-rank subspaces. Relative to a reference flag, it combines soft Schubert incidence evidence with Littlewood--Richardson products to retain geometric relations that may be lost by a single Euclidean or Grassmannian prototype.

The current work establishes basis and permutation invariance, finite-dimensionality independent of collection size, and perturbation guarantees. Computational evaluation is ongoing.

**Status:** Work in progress.
